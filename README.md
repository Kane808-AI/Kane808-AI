## How I work

- **Draft and surface, never execute.** Agents prepare, humans approve
  anything that sends, spends, publishes, or deletes.
- **Policy over vibes.** A deterministic rules layer decides what runs
  autonomously. Autonomy is a table, not a mood.
- **Verify before reporting done.** Self-reported completion without evidence
  gets sent back, by design, at the system level.
- **Regulated environments are the proving ground.** If the design survives
  attorney-client privilege, it survives your compliance review.
